The Last Shift

Characters:
Mara Quinn, 28-30, an ER doctor, the female lead.
Jun Park, 30, the man who left her, the male lead.
Ivy Chen, 27, Jun's assistant, supporting.

Episode 1

Scene 1. Emergency room, day.
Dusk. Low sun through the blinds as stretchers roll in. Mara meets the first one.
Dr. Quinn (shouting): Bed three, charge the paddles!
Nurses: On it!
The long tone of a heart monitor. A nurse drops a tray.

Scene 2. Hospital entrance, night.
Rain. Jun waits under an umbrella.
Jun: Mara. I'm back.
Mara (cold): It was raining when you left, too.

Episode 2

Scene 1. Jun's office, day.
Jun stares at a missed call on his phone. Ivy waits by the door.
Ivy: Mr. Park, she still won't answer.
Quinn (voice over): Stop calling me.
